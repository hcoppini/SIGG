## 2. Root Cause Analysis: Why the Scanner Locked Today

| Layer | Flaw Identified | Root Cause Mechanism | Resolution Applied |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Layer 1: Regime Logic** (`stock_scanner.py`) | Flawed boolean `OR` on lagging breadth | Evaluated `not index_above_sma20 or avg_mkt_ret <= -1.2`. Because the market had dropped for two weeks prior, market breadth (>SMA20) was 25.7%. The rule forced a **RED Kill Switch** despite a **+2.14%** market surge. | Added **Ignition Surge** detection: any session with `avg_mkt_ret >= +0.50%` immediately overrides lagging breadth and triggers **GREEN (Ignition Surge)**. |
| **Layer 2: Frontend Override** (`main.js` / `mobile.js`) | Frontend locked podium cards independently | The JavaScript check `(podiumData.is_locked \|\| regStr === 'RED')` ignored backend overrides. Even when the backend cleared `runner_surge` for allocation, the UI wiped the cards and rendered the locked banner. | Changed to check `podiumData.is_locked` authoritatively. When `runner_surge` is active, the podium displays candidate cards. |
| **Layer 3: Route & Scheduler** (`app.py`) | Defaulted strategy to `breakout_trend` and silenced fixing alerts | Background endpoints and fixing cron checked `regime != 'RED'`, silently dropping buy orders when the regime was stuck in red. | Synced all endpoints and scheduler hooks to `runner_surge` and permitted runner surge allocations during ignition sessions. |
